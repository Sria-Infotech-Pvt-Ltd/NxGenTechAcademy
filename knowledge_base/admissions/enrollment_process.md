# Admission & Enrollment Process at NxGen Tech Academy

1. **Inquiry & Demo:** Visitors can attend a weekly Free Live Demo class on Zoom or at Hyderabad campus.
2. **Profile Counseling:** 1-on-1 discussion on career goals and batch selection.
3. **Registration:** Submit application form with ID proof and complete enrollment.
4. **LMS Onboarding:** Immediate access to LMS portal, recorded classes, and project repositories.
