# SAP PP (Production Planning)

## Course Overview
End-to-end production planning and manufacturing execution training. Master BOMs, Routings, Work Centers, SOP, MRP Live, Discrete and Repetitive manufacturing in SAP S/4HANA.

## Course Description
Comprehensive 15-module manufacturing and production planning curriculum covering master data (BOMs, Routings, Work Centers, Production Versions), Sales & Operations Planning (SOP), Demand Management, PIR, MRP Live in S/4HANA, batch determination, variant configuration, Available-to-Promise (ATP), capacity planning, production order execution, discrete vs. repetitive manufacturing, backflushing, cross-module integration (MM, SD, QM, EWM, FI), and Make-to-Stock (MTS) / Make-to-Order (MTO) business scenarios.
