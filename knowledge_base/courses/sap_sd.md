# SAP SD (Sales & Distribution)

## Course Overview
Comprehensive sales and distribution training covering Order-to-Cash (O2C), pricing conditions, credit management (FSCM), shipping, billing, and S/4HANA Sales innovations.

## Course Description
Exhaustive 19-module functional sales and distribution curriculum covering enterprise sales structures (Sales Org, Distribution Channel, Division, Sales Area), customer master/Business Partner (CVI), partner determination, sales documents, copy control, pricing condition techniques (access sequences, condition records, calculation schema), availability check (ATP), shipping and route determination, Post Goods Issue (PGI), billing, FSCM credit management, BRF+ output management, sales returns, third-party/intercompany sales, transportation logistics, SD user exits/BAdIs/IDocs, and full project lifecycle implementation.
