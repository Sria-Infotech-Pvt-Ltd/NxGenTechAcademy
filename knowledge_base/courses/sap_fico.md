# SAP FICO Training

## Course Overview
Comprehensive training covering SAP Financial Accounting (FI) and Controlling (CO) integrated with S/4HANA Finance. Master General Ledger, AP, AR, Asset Accounting, Bank Accounting, Cost Center Accounting, Internal Orders, Product Costing, and CO-PA.

## Course Description
Exhaustive 21-module functional curriculum covering the entire enterprise finance architecture: ERP structure, FI global settings, GL accounting, Accounts Payable (AP), Accounts Receivable (AR), Asset Accounting (AA), Bank Accounting with electronic bank statements, Tax configuration (GST), Controlling (CO) cost center, internal orders, profit center, product costing, profitability analysis (CO-PA), MM-FI and SD-FI integration, S/4HANA Universal Journal (ACDOCA), Business Partner (CVI), and real-time business closing scenarios.
